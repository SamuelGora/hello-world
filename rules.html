Rules & Code of Conduct

These guidelines keep Arcadia Clash competitive, fair, and respectful. Review before registering or joining a team.

Eligibility

  • Players must be 16+ or have guardian consent.
  • Regional eligibility is based on residency.
  • Smurfing or account sharing is prohibited.

Roster rules

  • Teams field 4 starters and up to 2 substitutes.
  • Roster lock occurs 48 hours before the event.
  • Emergency swaps require admin approval.

Match operations

  • Check-in opens 60 minutes before match time.
  • Default wins occur after 15 minutes of no-show.
  • Disconnections must be reported within 5 minutes.

Streaming & media

  • Official streams have broadcast priority.
  • Players may stream with a 2-minute delay.
  • Clips and highlights must credit Arcadia Clash.

Code of conduct

  • Respect opponents, staff, and spectators.
  • No hate speech, harassment, or griefing.
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct results in penalties.

Penalties

  • Warnings for minor violations.
  • Match forfeits for repeated offenses.
  • Season bans for severe misconduct.

Need clarification?

Contact tournament staff or submit a ticket for review.

Play fair, play fierce

Thanks for helping us keep Arcadia Clash competitive and welcoming.
