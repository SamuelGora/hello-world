Frequently asked questions

Everything players and organizers need to know about joining, streaming, and competing in Arcadia Clash events.

How do I register as a solo player?

Head to the tournament sign up page and choose the individual player form. We will match free agents into balanced squads based on region and role preference.

When do brackets go live?

Brackets publish within 48 hours of registration closing. Follow the seeding page for updates on locks and bracket type.

What equipment is required?

Splatoon 3 events require a Nintendo Switch, stable broadband, and voice chat access via Discord or in-game comms.

Can I update my roster?

Teams can update starters and substitutes until roster lock. After that, only emergency substitutions approved by admins are allowed.

Where do I watch the stream?

Live broadcasts are available on Twitch. Use the live stream card on the front page or the schedule list to jump into the current match.

Who can I contact for support?

Reach us anytime at support@arcadiaclash.gg or through the Discord server linked in the footer of each page.

Still need help?

Join the live support queue and we will help you get match-ready.
